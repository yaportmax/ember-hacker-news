## Description

Ember is a minimal Hacker News reader for iPhone and iPad.

Browse Top, New, Best, Ask HN, Show HN, and Jobs. Read threaded discussions, collapse conversations, and open articles in a built-in browser with Reader support.

Find an old favorite with full-text search. Save stories for later, search your bookmarks, and pick up where you left off with reading history.

• Native design with light and dark themes
• Search with date filters and relevance or recent sorting
• Collapsible comments and author profiles
• Bookmarks and reading history stored on your device
• Cached feeds when you’re offline
• Adjustable density and system text sizing
• No ads, analytics, or account required to read

Voting, replying, submitting, and account sign-in open the official Hacker News website. External articles and full comment trees require an internet connection. Offline access covers cached feeds and saved story details, including loaded post text.

Ember is an independent app and is not affiliated with Y Combinator. Search is provided by Algolia.

## Review notes

No account is needed for the app’s native reading features. The app uses the public Hacker News API and Algolia search. For testing, open Top, select a story, expand replies, save the story with the bookmark button, and open the Saved tab. Search supports relevance/recent sorting and date ranges.

Account actions use the official Hacker News website through Apple's browser. The app does not handle credentials or offer in-app purchases. Public community content can be reported using the original item on HN and the publisher’s configured support page. Users can block authors from a profile, a comment menu, or the report page. Blocked content is hidden locally.

The release build uses live data. Debug-only UI-test fixtures are not compiled into the release app.
