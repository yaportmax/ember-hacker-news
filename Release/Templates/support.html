Privacy

Ember support

A minimal Hacker News reader for iPhone and iPad.

Get help

Email {{support_email}}. Include your device, iOS version, Ember version, and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help. Never send passwords.

Report content

For abusive content, send the Hacker News item or comment URL and a short explanation to {{support_email}}. You can also open the original item on Hacker News and use its flag action when available. Block an author through their profile or a comment’s menu to hide their content locally.

Bookmarks and offline reading

Swipe left on a story or tap the bookmark in a discussion. Bookmarks are local and separate from HN favorites. Saved story details and recent cached feeds remain available offline. External articles and full comment trees need a connection.

Accounts

Reading needs no account. To vote, reply, or submit a story, sign in through the official Hacker News website. Ember does not handle account credentials.

Connection issues

Pull down to refresh and check your connection. Search uses Algolia and can be unavailable independently of the HN feed. Retry after a short wait.
