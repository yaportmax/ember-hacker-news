Privacy

Updated {{date}} · {{publisher}}

Ember is an independent Hacker News reader. The app has no advertising SDKs, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated server. It does not request your contacts, location, photos, or Hacker News password.

Local data

Bookmarks, reading history, blocked users, hidden stories, and preferences are stored on your device. Recent feeds are cached. Device backups may include local app data according to your backup settings. You can remove bookmarks, history, and cached feeds in the app’s Settings. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data; copies in your device backups follow your backup provider’s retention settings.

Public data and search

The app requests stories, comments, and profiles from the public Hacker News API hosted by Firebase. Search text is sent to Algolia’s Hacker News search service. These providers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, and process requests under their own policies. No unique advertising identifier or app account is sent by Ember.

Websites and accounts

Opening an article contacts that website through Apple’s browser. Websites can use cookies and collect information under their own policies. HN sign-in, votes, replies, and submissions happen on Hacker News. Ember does not read passwords or browser cookies.
