# Ember support

Ember is a minimal Hacker News reader for iPhone and iPad, published by Max Yaport.

[Get help or report a problem](https://github.com/yaportmax/ember-hacker-news/issues/new) · [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)

For a bug, include your device, iOS version, Ember build number, and steps to reproduce it. GitHub issues are public; do not include passwords or private account information.

## Reading and discussions

Tap a story title to open its article. Tap the comments in the story's metadata to open the discussion. Stories without an external article open their discussion. Tap a comment to collapse it, and tap its header to expand it. Links and author buttons act independently. The small down arrow on a long comment moves to the next visible comment or reply.

Open the feed menu beside the large title for feeds, historical periods, custom dates, and additional Hacker News lists. Settings → Text & spacing has separate story and comment controls with live previews.

## Bookmarks and offline access

Use a story's menu or the bookmark in a discussion to save it. Bookmarks and history are stored on this device; they are separate from your Hacker News favorites. Cached feeds and saved story details can be read offline. External articles and full discussions need an internet connection.

## Report content

Use a comment's menu or author profile to block an author locally. Use Report to open the original Hacker News item and flag it when available, or [report the item to Ember support](https://github.com/yaportmax/ember-hacker-news/issues/new). Include the original item or comment URL and a short explanation. Blocked authors can be managed in Settings → Hidden content.

## Accounts and connections

No account is needed for native reading. Sign-in, voting, replying, and submitting open the official Hacker News website. Ember does not handle passwords.

Pull down to refresh after a connection problem. Search uses Algolia and may be unavailable independently of Hacker News. Previously loaded comments remain readable if loading is interrupted.
