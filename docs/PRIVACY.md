# Ember privacy policy

Updated September 13, 2026. Published by Max Yaport.

Ember is an independent Hacker News reader for iPhone and iPad. It has no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking, or developer-operated server. It does not request your contacts, location, photos, or Hacker News password.

## Data on your device

Bookmarks, reading history, blocked authors, hidden stories, and preferences are stored on your device. Recent feeds are cached. Your device backup settings may include this local data. You can remove bookmarks, history, and cached feeds in Settings. Uninstalling removes the app's local data; copies in backups follow your backup provider's retention settings.

## Public content and search

Stories, comments, and profiles come from the public Hacker News API hosted by Firebase. Additional feeds come from the Hacker News website. Search and historical feeds use Algolia's Hacker News search service. Your search text and selected search filters are sent to Algolia to return results.

These providers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, under their own policies. Ember does not send an advertising identifier or create an app account. The developer does not receive your searches, bookmarks, reading history, or app preferences.

## Articles and accounts

Opening an article contacts that website through Apple's browser or your chosen external browser. Websites may use cookies and collect information under their own policies. Signing in, voting, replying, and submitting stories take place on the official Hacker News website. Ember does not read your password or browser cookies.

## Support

You can contact the developer through [Ember support](https://github.com/yaportmax/ember-hacker-news/issues). GitHub issues are public: include only information you want to share publicly, and never include passwords or private account information. GitHub handles your GitHub account and issue data under its privacy policy. The developer uses information you choose to submit to respond to your request and resolve problems. You can edit your own issue or contact the developer through the issue about removing information.
