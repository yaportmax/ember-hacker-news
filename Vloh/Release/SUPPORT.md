# Vloh support
Contact [yaportmax@gmail.com](mailto:yaportmax@gmail.com) for help, privacy requests, or content concerns. Include your Vloh version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce a problem. Do not include passwords.

Vloh has three tabs: Groups, Record, and Settings. Each private group contains its own chat, vlog history, schedule, members, and group management. The Record tab resumes your current scheduled draft and opens the camera directly. Stop a clip, then record again on the same screen; clips save automatically. Review your draft to reorder, trim, import eligible footage, or post.

Only the scheduled person can post one vlog for a day. Post it that day or the following calendar day. Footage must be filmed between 2 AM on the vlog day and 8 AM the next morning, using the group's timezone. Imports need an original filming date. Order changes apply starting tomorrow.

Sharing requires iCloud. Members receive Apple's private CloudKit invite link. TestFlight installation invitations and group invitations are separate. Group owners can manage participants using Apple's sharing sheet.

Use a vlog menu or message context menu to report content, hide it, or block the author. Reports open Mail and are submitted only when you send them. You may choose to attach video evidence. Blocked members are managed in Settings. Group owners can remove content and revoke membership.

Open Settings → Your account to edit your name/photo, connect Apple sign-in, sign out, or delete your Vloh account/data. Deleting an owned group removes it for all members. Other people's exported copies cannot be recalled.
